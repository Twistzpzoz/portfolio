Case study 02Digital marketing campaign

Garmin Luna

“Every phase, every pace.” A three-month digital campaign for Luna, a concept women's smartwatch from Garmin Thailand, built around understanding her body, not just tracking it.

My role
Team member, campaign planning
Team
Team Black Market · 6 members
Context
Digital Marketing course (CSX4618/ITX4504), 2026
Channels
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LINE OA, SEO, Google Ads
§ 01

The brief

Garmin is a global leader in GPS and wearables, trusted for precision and competing directly with Apple Watch and Fitbit. But its fitness reputation has historically been performance-first, not woman-first.

Our brief was to launch Luna, a concept smartwatch designed for women, in Thailand. Its features include AI cycle-aware tracking with a hormonal readiness score, sleep, stress and recovery insights, a phase-aware training coach and Private Circles for safety sharing during solo workouts.

  • Price: 10,900–12,900 THB, positioned above Garmin Lily and below Venu 3.
  • Place: Garmin.com Thailand, Central Online and Power Buy, plus pop-up sampling at gyms and malls in month three.
Instead of showing women more data, Luna helps them understand what the data means.
§ 02

My role

This was a six-person team project for the Digital Marketing course. Together we took the campaign from brand analysis to a live Facebook launch.

As one of six members, I contributed to the campaign from the research stage through to the live launch.

§ 03

The process

  1. Find the audience

    Women aged 18–55 in Thailand with a monthly income above 30,000 THB who are active, wellness-focused and interested in wearables.

  2. Meet Kwan

    Our buyer persona: Kwan Suwannarat, 29, a marketing manager in Bangkok training for the Bangkok Marathon. Her fitness band keeps dying mid-run, and she needs a watch that survives Bangkok heat and long training days. We mapped her journey from awareness to advocacy.

  3. One big idea

    “Every Phase, Every Pace”: one idea and one voice across every channel. Understand her body, know how she feels, feel more confident. Brand voice: empowering, supportive, intelligent, trustworthy, inclusive.

  4. Five content pillars

    Cycle Syncing, Move at Your Pace, Data You Feel, Real Rhythm (community proof) and Know More, Not Just Log More, turning raw data into daily meaning.

  5. Channel plan

    Instagram for brand and community (4–5 posts a week plus Stories), TikTok for awareness (3–4 videos a week), Facebook for longer-form education (3 posts a week), and LINE OA for CRM. All planned in a 4-week content calendar.

  6. Segments and creatives

    Segment A, performance women aged 18–40: “Your Body Has a Rhythm. Luna Understands It.” Segment B, perimenopause women aged 40–55: “Your body is changing. Your technology should understand it.” Safety message: “Run Alone. Never Feel Alone.”

  7. Search, budget and testing

    On-page SEO and Google Ads groups for women's smartwatch, women's health and perimenopause keywords. A 20,000 THB monthly paid budget, three A/B tests on message, safety and visuals, and a weekly five-metric dashboard.

§ 04

The results

We set measurable goals for the first three months and built the launch assets to go after them:

300Ktarget impressions among women aged 18–55
5%target engagement rate on Instagram and TikTok
2Ktarget LINE OA subscribers
10Ktarget landing-page visits
200target qualified product leads

These are the campaign's planned targets, not measured results: the campaign was a course project for a concept product.

We then launched a real Facebook page for Garmin Luna, with our first posts, a product video and a customer feedback video, to test the creative direction in public.
