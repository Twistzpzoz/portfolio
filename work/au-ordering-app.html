Case study 05Product design · UI/UX

AU ordering app

Ordering and paying on campus, redesigned to be quick enough to use while standing in a queue.

My role
UI/UX designer
Format
Mobile app design
Context
Assumption University
Tools
Figma, user testing
Order
& payFigmaUI/UX
§ 01

The problem

Ordering and paying on campus should take seconds, but in practice it often means waiting. The goal was a mobile experience quick enough to use while standing in a queue.

§ 02

My role

I designed the ordering and payment screens and built the interactive prototypes in Figma. I also ran the user testing with students and turned their feedback into design changes.

§ 03

The process

  1. Design the core screens

    Designed the ordering and payment screens in Figma.

  2. Prototype

    Interactive Figma prototypes so students could try the flow rather than just look at it.

  3. Test and iterate

    User testing with students. Feedback was analysed and the design improved over several rounds.

§ 04

The results

A tested, interactive prototype of the ordering and payment flow, refined over several rounds of student feedback.
