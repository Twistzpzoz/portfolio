Hotel booking plan

A complete plan for a proposed cloud-based hotel reservation system, from charter and scope to sprints and payback.

My role
Project planner
Format
Project plan & feasibility study
Context
IT project management course
Methods
Agile/Scrum, WBS, Gantt, PERT, RACI
Scope,
sprint,
shipWBS · Gantt · RACIPM
§ 01

The problem

A hotel reservation platform touches guests, front-desk staff, payments and room inventory at once. Before anything is built, the project needs a clear scope, a realistic schedule, clear ownership and proof that it's worth the money.

§ 02

My role

I planned the project end to end: defining what would be built, scheduling it, assigning responsibility, and testing whether it was feasible.

§ 03

The process

  1. Define it

    Wrote the project charter and scope, then broke the work down into a work breakdown structure (WBS).

  2. Schedule it

    Built Gantt and PERT schedules to sequence the work.

  3. Own it

    Created a RACI matrix so every deliverable had someone responsible, accountable, consulted and informed.

  4. Stress-test it

    Ran a SWOT analysis plus technical, economic and operational feasibility studies, including ROI, NPV and payback period.

  5. Run it in sprints

    Organised delivery using Agile/Scrum.

§ 04

The results

A complete, decision-ready project plan: charter, scope, WBS, schedules, RACI, SWOT and a financial feasibility case built on ROI, NPV and payback.
